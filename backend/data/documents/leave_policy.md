# Xiarch Bharat Comprehensive Leave & Time-Off Policy

## 1. Annual Leave (Paid Time Off)
Full-time permanent employees are allocated 24 days of paid annual leave per financial year (April 1 to March 31). Leave accrues at 2 days per full month of active service.
- **Carry Forward:** Up to 10 days of unused annual leave can be carried forward into the next calendar year. Any excess days will expire unless an official carryover exception is granted by the Department Head.
- **Notice Period:** For leaves exceeding 3 consecutive days, employees must submit requests at least 5 business days in advance.
- **Blackout Periods:** Key release weeks or quarterly closing weeks may have restricted approvals subject to team manager discretion.

## 2. Sick & Medical Leave
- Employees receive 12 days of sick leave annually.
- Medical certificates signed by a registered medical practitioner are mandatory when sick leave spans 3 or more consecutive business days.
- Hospitalization or critical illness may trigger extended medical coverage subject to insurance provisions.

## 3. Casual & Emergency Leave
- Employees receive 8 days of casual leave intended for urgent personal affairs, religious celebrations, or domestic contingencies.
- Casual leave cannot be clubbed with annual leave exceeding 2 consecutive days without manager approval.

## 4. Discrepancy & Leave Audit Policy
If an employee's self-reported leave balance differs from the HR central database:
- The central SQLite database is considered the primary system of record.
- In case of a claimed discrepancy, the HR Operations desk verifies attendance punch logs and approved leave slips to reconcile any discrepancy within 3 business days.
