# Xiarch Bharat Hybrid & Remote Work Policy

## 1. Eligibility & Model
Xiarch Bharat champions flexibility while fostering collaborative innovation.
- **Engineering, Product, and Design:** Eligible for a hybrid structure permitting up to 3 remote days per week.
- **Mandatory In-Office Days:** Tuesday and Thursday are core synchronous days for cross-functional sprint planning and team architecture reviews.
- **Optional Remote Fridays:** All eligible departments have pre-approved optional remote work on Fridays unless critical client deliveries demand physical co-location.

## 2. Infrastructure & Equipment Allowance
- A one-time ergonomic work-from-home setup grant of INR 25,000 is provided to full-time employees upon passing probation.
- A recurring high-speed internet reimbursement of INR 2,000 per month is processed via expense claims with valid broadband receipts.

## 3. Core Working Hours & Availability
- Core availability hours are 10:00 AM to 5:00 PM IST across Slack, Google Meet, and email.
- Emergency pager duty and on-call rotations for DevOps/SRE follow designated shift calendars with compensatory time-off.
