# Xiarch Bharat Sabbatical & Professional Development Policy

## 1. Sabbatical Leave Eligibility
Xiarch Bharat values continuous learning and lifelong growth. Sabbatical leaves allow staff to recharge, engage in research, or pursue formal academic degrees.

### Eligibility Criteria:
1. **Tenure Requirement:** Minimum 5 continuous years of full-time service with the organization.
2. **Performance History:** Must maintain a performance rating of 'Exceeds Expectations' or 'Consistently Meets Expectations' in the preceding two annual appraisal cycles.
3. **Absence of Disciplinary Flags:** No open compliance or HR disciplinary notices.

### Duration & Compensation:
- Sabbaticals are sanctioned between 1 to 3 months.
- First month is paid at 50% basic salary; months 2 and 3 are unpaid leave with continued health insurance benefits.
- Requires VP and Executive leadership written endorsement at least 60 days in advance.

## 2. Professional Certification & Higher Education
- Annual learning budget of INR 60,000 per engineer for cloud certifications (AWS, GCP, Azure, CKA), cybersecurity credentials, or AI engineering courses.
- 100% exam fee reimbursement on passing accredited certification assessments.
